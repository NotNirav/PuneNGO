# NGOConnect — Bridging Pune With Grassroots Impact 🤝

> A community-led initiative making it effortless for anyone in Pune to discover, support, and connect directly with local non-governmental organizations.

---

## 📖 The Story Behind NGOConnect

Across Pune, countless non-profit organizations and volunteer collectives work tirelessly every day. They feed street animals, teach underprivileged children in bastis, plant native trees on city hills, clean the Mula–Mutha riverbanks, and support senior citizens in old-age homes.

Yet, a glaring disconnect exists in our city:

- **Passionate citizens** want to donate old clothes, teach on weekends, or contribute money, but they simply do not know which NGOs exist in their immediate neighbourhood or how to reach them.
- **Grassroots NGOs** are deeply focused on their ground missions and often lack websites, advertising budgets, or digital media teams to make themselves easily discoverable.

**NGOConnect was created to bridge this gap.**

Developed as a **Design Thinking** project by second-year engineering students at **Pimpri Chinchwad College of Engineering (PCCOE), Pune**, NGOConnect re-imagines community giving as a fast, honest, and transparent experience.

---

## 💡 What is NGOConnect?

NGOConnect is a free public directory of NGOs operating across Pune and Pimpri-Chinchwad. It serves as a digital bridge where you can:

1. **Search by what you care about**: Find organizations working in education, animal welfare, environmental conservation, health and medicine, child welfare, hunger relief, and senior care.
2. **Find help near you**: Browse NGOs across 28 Pune localities—from Kothrud, Baner, and Aundh to Hinjewadi, Nigdi, and Hadapsar—or sort automatically by distance to find organizations right around your corner.
3. **Connect in one tap**: Call, WhatsApp, email, or get Google Maps directions to the NGO directly. There are no registration forms, no payment commissions, and no waiting.

## 🌿 Our Guiding Principles

### 1. Zero Middlemen
When you want to help, nothing should stand between you and the organization. Every profile gives you direct telephone numbers, verified WhatsApp links, physical addresses, and official social media pages.

### 2. We Never Handle Money
NGOConnect is purely an informational directory. We do not collect donations, we do not take cuts or commissions, and we do not process transactions. When you choose to donate, you contribute directly to the NGO’s official bank account or UPI link.

### 3. Always 100% Free
Getting listed is free for every genuine NGO, and browsing the directory is free for every citizen. We do not accept paid promotions, sponsored rankings, or premium listing tiers.

### 4. Privacy & Simplicity First
You do not need to create an account, register your email, or download an app to use NGOConnect. Finding a local cause should be as simple and frictionless as opening a phonebook.

## 🎨 Built Through Design Thinking

NGOConnect was crafted through the five core stages of the Design Thinking methodology at PCCOE:

1. **Empathize**: We interviewed everyday Pune residents, college students, and NGO representatives to understand how people discover local social causes and why charitable intent often does not translate into action.
2. **Define**: We identified the core friction points: confusing middlemen platforms taking commissions, lack of transparent contact numbers, and absence of neighbourhood-specific discovery.
3. **Ideate**: We brainstormed a direct, search-first platform tailored specifically to Pune’s geography, featuring instant one-tap contact buttons and clear cause classifications.
4. **Prototype**: We designed a warm, accessible visual identity using friendly, inviting tones (Cobalt Blue, Warm Peach, Soft Ice, and Fresh Lime) along with mobile-optimized profiles that put essential contact buttons front and center.
5. **Test**: We tested the portal with fellow students and community volunteers, gathering feedback to refine the search experience and make NGO onboarding as simple as sending a WhatsApp message.

## 📝 How NGOs Get Listed

Any genuine NGO operating in Pune can request a free profile in minutes:

1. **Send Details**: The NGO shares basic information (name, causes, location, short description, and contact number) via WhatsApp or email using our pre-formatted templates.
2. **Review & Preparation**: The team organizes the profile, verifies public details, and arranges photos.
3. **Live Profile**: The NGO is published to the directory, making them discoverable to thousands of citizens looking to volunteer or donate.
